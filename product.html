The Meaning

An ornament with something to say

In the South-Indian tradition, jewellery was never merely worn — it was read. The goddess for prosperity, the peacock for grace, the mango for fertility and abundance. To wear these motifs is to carry an intention, not just an accessory.

This piece belongs to that lineage: designed in our Chennai studio, hand-finished in antique gold, and made to mean something each time you fasten it.

Craftsmanship

The hands behind the piece

Each ornament passes through five stages — design, sourcing, setting, polishing and inspection — most of them by hand. Our karigars set every stone individually and finish each surface in warm antique gold, so the piece arrives with the depth of an heirloom.
