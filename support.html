Orders & Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, all major credit and debit cards, and Net Banking through our secure payment partner. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for in-stock pieces shipping within India. Made-to-order commissions are prepaid, as each is crafted specifically for you.
Is Cash on Delivery available?
Yes, COD is offered on in-stock, ready-to-ship pieces delivered within India. Because made-to-order pieces are crafted to commission, they are not eligible for COD and are confirmed with prepayment.
Will I receive an order confirmation?
You will receive an email confirmation as soon as your order is placed, followed by a dispatch note with tracking once your piece leaves our Chennai studio. You are welcome to reach us on WhatsApp at any point for an update.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
For in-stock pieces, write to us within 24 hours and we will do our best to amend or cancel before dispatch. Made-to-order pieces enter the workshop shortly after confirmation, so changes may not be possible once crafting has begun — please reach out as soon as you can.

Shipping

How long will my order take to arrive?
In-stock pieces are dispatched within India and typically arrive in 2–4 working days. Made-to-order pieces are crafted by hand and ship in approximately 2–3 weeks. Full timelines are in the Shipping section below.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We ship worldwide via insured express courier, with most international deliveries arriving in 7–12 days after dispatch. Any customs duties or import taxes levied by the destination country are payable by the recipient.
Is my order insured in transit?
Every shipment is fully insured and sent through a tracked, signature-on-delivery service, both within India and internationally, so your piece is protected until it reaches your hands.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?
In-stock pieces may be returned within 7 days of delivery, unworn and in their original packaging with all tags intact. Made-to-order pieces and earrings (for reasons of hygiene) are not eligible for return. See the full steps in the Shipping & Returns section.
How do I start a return?
Write to us at care@ahankaraka.com or message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with your order number. We will share a return label and guide you through the rest. Once your piece is received and inspected, your refund is issued to the original payment method.
How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 3–5 working days of our receiving and inspecting the returned piece. Depending on your bank or card issuer, the amount may take a few additional days to reflect in your account.

Products & Materials

Is this real gold?
We believe in honesty above all. Our pieces are not solid gold. They are crafted in a skin-safe, nickel-free alloy and finished with a rich antique-gold polish, in the tradition of temple jewellery. This is a deliberate, conscious choice — it lets us offer heirloom-worthy craftsmanship and scale of design at a fraction of the cost and ecological weight of solid gold.
Will the pieces irritate sensitive skin?
Our base alloy is nickel-free and chosen for its skin-friendliness, so it suits most wearers, including those with sensitive skin. As with any jewellery, we recommend keeping pieces dry and away from perfumes and lotions to preserve both the finish and your comfort.
Are the stones genuine?
We use carefully selected synthetic and semi-precious stones, hand-set in the temple style. Each product page lists exactly what a given design contains. Where a piece features a particular stone, it is always described honestly.
Can I commission a custom or bespoke piece?
Yes. Many of our designs can be adapted, and we also take on bespoke commissions for weddings and special occasions. Begin a conversation through our contact page and our studio will guide you from sketch to finished piece.

Care Guide

Made to be worn, and kept

Temple jewellery is meant to be lived in. A little mindful care keeps the antique-gold finish luminous for years.

01

Maintenance

Let your jewellery be the last thing you put on and the first you take off. Keep it away from perfume, hairspray, lotions and water, which dull the polish over time. Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after each wear.

02

Storage

Store each piece individually in the soft pouch or box it arrived in, away from humidity and direct sunlight. Keeping pieces separate prevents the stones and finish from scratching one another.

03

Cleaning

For everyday freshening, a dry microfibre cloth is all you need. Avoid liquid cleaners, ultrasonic machines and abrasive materials, which can lift the antique-gold finish. When a piece needs more, send it to our studio.

A few do's and don'ts

Do remove your jewellery before bathing, swimming, sleeping, exercising or cooking. Do put it on after your fragrance and make-up have settled. Do wipe it down with a soft dry cloth before storing.

Don't let pieces touch water, perfume, sweat or harsh chemicals for long. Don't store pieces loose or stacked together. Don't use toothpaste, baking soda or chemical dips, which strip the finish.

Size Guide

Finding your fit

Bangles are sized by inner diameter. Necklaces and earrings are crafted to a considered one-size, with adjustable lengths where the design allows.

To find your bangle size, take a bangle that already fits you well and measure its inner diameter across the widest point in inches. If you are sizing from your hand, gently press your fingers together as if slipping on a bangle, then measure around the widest part of your hand and divide that circumference by 3.14 to find the diameter.

Size Inner Diameter (inch) Inner Diameter (cm) Best suited to
2.2 2.2" 5.6 cm Petite hands
2.4 2.4" 6.1 cm Small to medium hands
2.6 2.6" 6.6 cm Medium hands (most popular)
2.8 2.8" 7.1 cm Larger hands

Necklaces are finished at a considered standard length suited to most necklines, with an adjustable extension chain where the design permits; earrings are a single one-size. If a piece can be lengthened or shortened, it is noted on its product page.

Shipping & Returns

Every piece is dispatched from our Chennai studio, insured and tracked, and packed to be opened with a little ceremony.

In-stock pieces leave our studio quickly, while made-to-order pieces are crafted by hand especially for you and therefore take a little longer — a wait we think is always worth it. All shipments, within India and internationally, are fully insured and require a signature on delivery.

Returns, step by step

1. Write to us at care@ahankaraka.com or message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery, quoting your order number.
2. We will confirm eligibility and email you a prepaid return label.
3. Pack the piece unworn, with tags intact, in its original box and pouch.
4. Hand it to the courier; once it reaches us, we inspect it within 1–2 working days.
5. Your refund is issued to the original payment method within 3–5 working days of inspection.

Eligibility: Returns apply to in-stock pieces only. Made-to-order pieces are excluded as they are crafted to commission, and earrings are excluded for reasons of hygiene. Pieces showing signs of wear cannot be accepted.

Warranty & Repairs

Cared for, for years

We stand behind every piece that leaves our studio, and we would rather mend than discard.

Every AHANKARAKA piece carries a one-year polish warranty from the date of delivery. Under normal, careful wear, we will restore the antique-gold finish should it fade or tarnish within that year, at no cost to you beyond two-way insured shipping. The warranty covers the finish and the integrity of the craftsmanship; it does not cover loss, accidental damage, or wear arising from contact with water, perfume or chemicals.

Repair & re-polishing process

1. Write to us at care@ahankaraka.com with your order number and a few clear photographs of the piece.
2. Our studio assesses the piece and confirms whether it falls within warranty or is a chargeable repair.
3. We share a prepaid label and you send the piece to our Chennai studio, fully insured.
4. We re-polish, re-set or repair as needed and return it to you, insured, usually within 2–3 weeks.

Beyond the first year, we are always happy to refresh and repair your jewellery on a chargeable basis — many of our pieces are meant to be passed on, and we believe in keeping them beautiful for the next wearer too.
