The Journal

Stories & Meaning

A piece of temple jewellery is never only an ornament — it is a vow cast in gold, a prayer worn close to the skin. We keep this journal because the stories behind our work deserve to be told slowly: the temple traditions of the South, the patient hands of our karigars in Chennai, and the lives our pieces go on to live. Less a magazine, more a quiet conversation about beauty that is made to be inherited.

Featured · Heritage

The Meaning of Temple Jewellery

Born in the sanctums of South India, temple jewellery began not as adornment but as devotion — gold offered to deities and later worn by devadasis as they danced. The goddess Lakshmi, the peacock, the sacred lotus: each motif carries a blessing older than memory. We trace how a craft conceived for the divine came to rest, generations later, against a bride's collarbone — and why wearing it still feels like carrying a small temple with you.
